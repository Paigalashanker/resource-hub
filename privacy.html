Privacy Policy

This policy explains how CampusVault handles information when you browse the site or contact its maintainer.

Last updated: September 14, 2026

Information we collect

CampusVault is primarily a static educational resource site. The site does not require an account. If you use the contact form, the name, email address, subject, and message you provide are sent to the form-processing service used by the site so the maintainer can respond.

Cookies and local storage

The site may use browser local storage for preferences, such as theme settings, cached resource data, and a local fallback visit count. These values remain in your browser and are not a CampusVault account. Your browser or third-party services may also use cookies according to their own policies.

Google services and future advertising

Some resources may open Google-hosted documents or other external services. Those services may process information under their own privacy policies. Google AdSense is not currently active on this website. If advertising is added in the future, advertising cookies or similar technologies may be used to deliver, measure, and limit advertising in accordance with Google’s policies and your available consent choices.

Analytics and third-party services

CampusVault does not claim to use an analytics service unless it is visibly integrated into the site. The site currently connects to services such as GitHub-hosted repository data, FormSubmit for the contact form, and external APIs for optional widgets. Their handling of data is governed by their respective policies.

How information is used

Information submitted through the contact form is used to read and answer your message, investigate site issues, and communicate about a request. It is not sold by CampusVault.

Retention and security

Messages may be retained by the contact-processing provider and the maintainer for as long as reasonably needed to handle the request. No online service can guarantee absolute security, so avoid sending passwords, payment details, or other sensitive information through the form.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information you submitted. Contact the maintainer to make a request.

Children’s privacy

CampusVault is intended for general educational use and does not knowingly request personal information from children. Please contact the maintainer if you believe a child has submitted personal information.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the site, services, or legal requirements change. The date above indicates the latest revision.

Contact

For privacy questions, email paigalashanker@gmail.com or use the contact page.
